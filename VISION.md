# Vision

## Purpose
DisasterProtekt exists to help public leaders make high-stakes decisions before emergencies unfold. It is a consequence simulator, not a dashboard. The system reveals what likely happens when action is taken—or delayed—so that policy choices are grounded in evidence and timeline-aware outcomes.

## Who It Helps
- City councils preparing for heat, flood, or grid stress events
- Emergency operations teams evaluating evacuation or shelter strategies
- Infrastructure leaders assessing system strain under compounding hazards

## What Makes It Different
- **Interactive, not static:** users manipulate decisions and watch outcomes evolve.
- **Multi-modal evidence:** visual simulations + quantitative timelines in one view.
- **Narrative reasoning:** Gemini 3 Pro explains why outcomes diverge, minute by minute.
- **Compounding reality:** layered hazards capture cascading failures, not single-silo events.

## North Star
Enable leaders to ask, “What if we act now? What if we wait?” and receive a clear, defensible, and comparative answer within minutes—before real people are put at risk.
